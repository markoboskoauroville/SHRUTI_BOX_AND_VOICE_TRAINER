Basic Drone Combinations

These are the foundational drone combinations used in Indian classical music. Click on any combination to activate it on the shruti box. Note: Some combinations (e.g., Sa-Ga) are non-standard and included for educational purposes.

Sa-Pa (C-G)

Beginner

The most basic and widely used drone combination. Creates a perfect fifth interval.

Used in: Most ragas, classical, folk, and devotional music.

Sa-Ma (C-F)

Beginner

Creates a perfect fourth interval. Stable and grounding.

Used in: Ragas where Ma is prominent, like Bageshri, Malkauns.

Sa-Sa' (C-C)

Beginner

Octave drone. Simple but effective for vocal practice.

Used in: Beginning vocal exercises, simple melodies.

Sa-Pa-Sa' (C-G-C)

Beginner

The most common traditional combination. Creates stability and fullness.

Used in: Most classical performances, standard tanpura tuning.

Sa-Ga (C-E)

Beginner

Creates a major third interval. Bright and uplifting. Note: Non-standard for traditional drones.

Used in: Experimental or light classical music with major tonality.

Intermediate Drone Combinations

These combinations add more color and complexity to the drone texture. Some are non-standard and included for educational purposes.

Sa-Ma-Pa (C-F-G)

Intermediate

Adds richness to the drone. Both perfect fourth and fifth intervals.

Used in: Ragas with prominent Ma like Bageshri, Malkauns.

Sa-Ma'-Pa (C-F#-G)

Intermediate

Used specifically for ragas with Tivra (sharp) Ma.

Used in: Ragas like Yaman, Puriya, Marwa.

Sa-Ni-Sa' (C-B-C)

Intermediate

Creates tension and resolution with the leading tone Ni. Note: Non-standard.

Used in: Experimental settings or ragas with strong Ni like Yaman.

Sa-ni-Sa' (C-A#-C)

Intermediate

For ragas with komal (flat) Ni. Note: Non-standard.

Used in: Experimental settings or ragas like Bhairavi.

Sa-ga-Pa (C-D#-G)

Intermediate

Adds a minor third for a more melancholic sound. Note: Non-standard.

Used in: Experimental settings or ragas with komal Ga like Bhairavi.

Sa-Pa-Ga (C-G-E)

Intermediate

Adds a major third for brighter tonality. Note: Non-standard.

Used in: Experimental settings or ragas with prominent major character.

Advanced Drone Combinations

These complex drone combinations create specific moods and are used for specialized ragas. Many are non-standard and included for educational purposes.

Sa-Pa-ni-Sa' (C-G-A#-C)

Advanced

Rich combination with perfect fifth and minor seventh. Note: Non-standard.

Used in: Experimental settings or complex ragas from Bhairavi family.

Sa-re-Pa (C-C#-G)

Advanced

Creates tension with the minor second (komal Re). Note: Non-standard.

Used in: Experimental settings or ragas like Shree.

Sa-Pa-Dha-Sa' (C-G-A-C)

Advanced

Adds major sixth for a richer texture. Note: Non-standard.

Used in: Experimental light classical forms.

Sa-ga-Ma-ni (C-D#-F-A#)

Advanced

Highly specialized drone for specific moods. Note: Non-standard.

Used in: Experimental settings or Bhairavi family ragas.

Sa-Re-Pa-Ni (C-D-G-B)

Advanced

Create a bright, complex sound with natural Re and Ni. Note: Non-standard.

Used in: Experimental settings or Kalyan family ragas.

Sa-Ma'-Dha-Sa' (C-F#-A-C)

Advanced

Creates a characteristic lydian sound with sharp Ma. Note: Non-standard.

Used in: Experimental settings or ragas like Puriya, Marwa.

Western Chord Combinations

Create familiar Western chord drones using the shruti box. Click on any chord to activate it.

C Major

The most basic major chord, bright and happy.

Notes: C-E-G (Sa-Ga-Pa)

C Minor

Sad, melancholic character with minor third.

Notes: C-Eb-G (Sa-ga-Pa)

G Major

Dominant chord in the key of C, creates tension.

Notes: G-B-D (Pa-Ni-Re)

F Major

Subdominant chord in the key of C, warm sound.

Notes: F-A-C (Ma-Dha-Sa)

A Minor

Relative minor of C major. Wistful quality.

Notes: A-C-E (Dha-Sa'-Ga)

D Minor

Used in many melancholic progressions.

Notes: D-F-A (Re-Ma-Dha)

C Suspended 4th

Ambiguous, neither major nor minor. Conveys tension.

Notes: C-F-G (Sa-Ma-Pa)

G Suspended 4th

Open sound, common in folk music.

Notes: G-C-D (Pa-Sa'-Re)

C Suspended 2nd

Bright and open. Has an unresolved quality.

Notes: C-D-G (Sa-Re-Pa)

C Major 7th

Jazzy, sophisticated sound with the 7th degree.

Notes: C-E-G-B (Sa-Ga-Pa-Ni)

C Minor 7th

Mellow, used in jazz and R&B.

Notes: C-Eb-G-Bb (Sa-ga-Pa-ni)

G Dominant 7th

Creates tension, naturally resolves to C.

Notes: G-B-D-F (Pa-Ni-Re-Ma)
